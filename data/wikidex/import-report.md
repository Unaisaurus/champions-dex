## How reliable WikiDex is

For X and Y onwards, the app has the games' own Spanish text. On these pages, WikiDex's entries for those games match it in 2869 of 2967 cases (96.7%, ignoring punctuation and case). Nearly all the differences are in X, Y, Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire, where WikiDex gives an older game's text that those games reworded.
